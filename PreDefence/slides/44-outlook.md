# Outlook

<div class="timeline"><div><b>Detector response</b><p>Tune digitisation, calibration and veto selections with prototype and beam-test measurements.</p></div><div><b>Rare-process simulation</b><p>Increase dedicated background statistics with improved sampling and more detailed uncertainty models.</p></div><div><b>Positron beam energies</b><p>Extend the analysis to additional design energies and optimise selections for each mass range.</p></div></div><div class="formula-band" style="margin-top:33px"><LaTeX formula="m_{A^{\prime}}^{\mathrm{ann}}\sim\sqrt{2m_eE_{e^+}}"/></div><div class="takeaway">Changing the positron beam energy shifts the annihilation mass range.</div>
