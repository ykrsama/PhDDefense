# Statistical and detector assumptions

<div class="columns"><div><h2 class="blue">Reported statistical treatment</h2><ul class="small"><li>Expected 90% CL, background-only hypothesis</li><li>Profile likelihood and asymptotic CLs</li><li>Shared signal strength for all production modes</li><li>Background-shape uncertainties uncorrelated by bin</li><li>Normalisation follows the reported Poisson prescription</li></ul></div><div><h2 class="gold">Further validation</h2><ul class="small"><li>Coverage with toy Monte Carlo at very low counts</li><li>Uncertainty from sideband continuation</li><li>Bounds on omitted empty-tail components</li><li>HCAL noise and pileup at the 1 MeV cell threshold</li><li>Signal efficiency and background stability with beam energy</li></ul></div></div><div class="takeaway">Mutually exclusive regions do not imply independent detector-systematic uncertainties.</div>
